The Last Train

11:47 PM.

The station is almost completely empty. You have been sitting on the same bench for nearly an hour, listening to the faint hum of the lights above you. The next train is supposed to be the last one of the night.

Then your phone vibrates.

There is one new message from an unknown number:

Don't get on the last train.

You look down the tracks. Nothing is there yet. The message has no name, no explanation, and no way to reply.

What do you do?

  • Read the message again and look for a clue.
  • Ignore the message and wait for the train.
