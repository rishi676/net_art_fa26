The Message

You read the message again.

Don't get on the last train.

There is something strange about it. The message was sent exactly at 11:47 PM, the same time displayed on the station clock. You check your phone again. There is no signal.

Then you notice something you missed before. A second line has appeared beneath the first.

Look under the bench.

You slowly kneel down. Taped underneath the bench is a small folded piece of paper. On it is a single sentence:

"You have been here before."

You cannot remember ever seeing this station before tonight.

You have two choices.

  1. Investigate the station and find out who left the message.
  2. Forget the message and wait for the train.
