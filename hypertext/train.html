The Train Arrives

At 11:58 PM, you hear the train before you see it.

A low sound rolls through the tunnel. The lights at the end of the platform flicker, and a train slowly appears from the darkness.

There are no advertisements on the walls. No other passengers are visible through the windows.

The train stops directly in front of you.

The doors open.

For a moment, you consider the message. Then you notice something that makes you hesitate: every seat inside the train is empty except for one.

A person is sitting near the back, facing away from you.

You can either step onto the train or stay on the platform.

  • Get on the train.
  • Stay on the platform and investigate what is happening.
