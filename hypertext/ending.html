The Ending

You step onto the train.

The doors close behind you without making a sound.

You look toward the person at the back of the train. Before you can speak, they turn around.

It is you.

Not exactly you as you are now, but an older version of yourself. They look tired, but strangely calm.

"You finally came," they say.

You look out the window. The station is disappearing into the darkness.

"What is this?" you ask.

The older version of you smiles.

"The last train is always the first train to somewhere else."

The lights go out.

The End.
