The Investigation

You decide not to leave yet.

You walk toward the end of the platform. The station feels different now. The lights are still humming, but the sound seems to be coming from somewhere below you.

Behind a support column, you find an old station map. One of the lines has been circled in pen. It leads to a platform that does not appear on the current station signs.

Under the map is another piece of paper.

If you are reading this, you chose to stay.

You turn around.

The train doors are still open. The person inside is now standing and looking directly at you.

Your phone vibrates one more time.

Last chance.

You realize that the choice was never really about the train. It was about whether you were willing to find out why someone wanted you here.

There is only one thing left to do.

Follow the person onto the train.

Return to the beginning.
